# `align-intent` — check understanding before acting

[中文](/skills/align-intent/README.md)｜**English**

Ask "Do you understand what I mean?" and the AI stops first, separates what you said from what it guessed, asks only the one question that changes the next step, and waits for your answer before going on. The full spec is in [`SKILL.md`](/skills/align-intent/SKILL.md), written in Traditional Chinese.
